About me

I grew up in Changzhou, China, which is where my curiosity about people and society started. I moved to the US to study journalism and began by covering social change, business and technology.

As a reporter I followed human-centered stories: pregnant women facing unequal systems, migrant workers and seafarers, elders whose lives were shaped by hardship. Some of those stories reunited long-lost friends, raised donations for an Afghan girl, and were included in a book by Contemporary World Press.

I co-founded a school serving underserved communities, then completed an MBA at Oxford. Today I work where sustainability, data and storytelling meet. I've lived and worked in Beijing, Shanghai, Austin, Houston, Washington D.C., San Francisco, Oxford, London and now Los Angeles.
